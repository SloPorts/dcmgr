Console-to-screen timing

A recorded frame appears a little after the DC drew it. This delay lines up console lines with the recording: clicking a line in a session shows the frame that delay later. Calibrating runs a short test program that flashes the screen and announces each flash on the console, then measures the lag.

Preview of the result (scaled to 640x480).

The screen shows the whole uncropped frame while you adjust (everyone watching sees it too; it ends by itself after 10 minutes). Drag the box or its handles, type values, or use the arrow keys on the box (Alt + arrows resize, Shift for bigger steps).
